SEO deployment notes
====================
Canonical URLs assume these HTML files are uploaded at the root of https://bold777games.com/ :
  index.html -> https://bold777games.com/
  about.html -> https://bold777games.com/about.html
  signup.html -> https://bold777games.com/signup.html
  login.html -> https://bold777games.com/login.html
  blog.html -> https://bold777games.com/blog.html

IMPORTANT: The live domain already has WordPress pages and a sitemap. Before deployment, verify public paths. If you publish in a folder or on another domain, change canonical URLs, og:url values and sitemap locations to match actual public URLs. Do not replace the current site's sitemap without merging URLs or submitting the new sitemap appropriately.

The empty Blog page has noindex,follow and is intentionally excluded from the sitemap. Once real posts are published, update its meta to index,follow and add its URL to your sitemap.

This ZIP is a static HTML website, not a WordPress theme. Uploading the ZIP directly as a WordPress theme will not work.
